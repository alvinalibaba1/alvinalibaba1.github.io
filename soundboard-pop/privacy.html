Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Soundboard Pop is a soundboard app for the Mac. This policy explains what happens to your information.

The short version

Soundboard Pop does not collect your personal information. Your boards, sounds and settings stay on your Mac.

What the app stores on your Mac

  • Your boards, tiles, keys and settings
  • The sound files you add or record
  • Automatic backups of these, in a folder on your Mac

This information never leaves your Mac unless you share a board file yourself. We cannot see it.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only when you turn on the Mic switch (to mix your voice with your sounds) or record a new sound. The audio is processed on your Mac. It is not sent to us or anyone else, and it is saved only if you choose to record a sound into a tile.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and gives the app a confirmation that Pro is unlocked. We do not receive your payment details or personal information. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchases.

No tracking, no ads, no accounts

The App Store version of Soundboard Pop has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no account or sign-in. It does not include any third-party code that collects data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
