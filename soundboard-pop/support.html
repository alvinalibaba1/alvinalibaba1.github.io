Frequently asked questions

My key doesn't work when another app is in front. Set "Keyboard keys work" at the bottom of the window to Everywhere. In Only in this app mode, keys work only while Soundboard Pop is focused.

The mic doesn't work. The app asks for microphone access only when you turn on the Mic switch or record a sound. If it says access is off, allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Can I play sounds into WhatsApp, Zoom or a call? Calls need a virtual audio device such as BlackHole to route the sounds. OBS can record the sounds with its own macOS audio capture.

What are the free limits? What does Pro add? Free includes 3 boards and 40 sounds. Pro unlocks unlimited boards and sounds, mic mixing, output routing, chains, stop groups, and pitch and speed. It is monthly, yearly (with a free trial) or a one-time Lifetime purchase.

How do I restore my Pro purchase? Open the Pro screen and choose Restore Purchase, signed in with the Apple ID you bought with. If nothing is found, the app shows a plain message.

Which audio files can I import? mp3, wav, m4a and aiff. Drag them onto the window or use the add button. Files are copied into the app's own storage.

Did I lose my boards? The app keeps automatic daily backups. If the saved file is damaged, it restores the newest readable backup and tells you. Your data never leaves your Mac. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I share a board? Yes. Export it as a .sbboard file and send it to another Soundboard Pop user.
