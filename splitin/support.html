Frequently asked questions

The scanner misread my receipt. Lay the receipt flat in good light and fill the frame. Every scanned item is editable, so fix any line, and Splitin checks your items against the printed total. You can also skip scanning and add items by hand.

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up. Your bills, friends and bank details stay on your phone.

How do I restore my Splitin Plus subscription? Open the Splitin Plus screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. You can also manage or cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

I'm not getting payment reminders. Reminders are local notifications. Make sure notifications are allowed for Splitin in iOS Settings → Notifications.

How do I delete my data? Bills and profile data live only on your phone and go away when you delete the app. To delete the device ID and scan activity kept on our server, email us with your device ID or a screenshot of the Profile screen. See the Privacy Policy.

Which languages are supported? English and Indonesian.

I found a bug or have an idea. Email us. Feedback is very welcome.
