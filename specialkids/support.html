Frequently asked questions

Is SpecialKids free? The first 4 themes of every game, the 3 picture stories and Free Play are free. Premium (monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers) opens every theme. Family Sharing is on.

How do I subscribe or restore my purchase? Press and hold the button for about 3 seconds to open the grown-ups area, then tap the gold star button on the game list to open the offer. Restore Purchase is on that same screen. Use the Apple ID you subscribed with. Children can never buy by accident.

How do I cancel? Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad and choose SpecialKids.

Can I turn off sounds and movement? Yes. In the grown-ups area you can switch music, sound effects, vibration, voice and reduced motion off one by one, or all at once with Quiet Mode.

Does the app collect data or need the internet? No account, no ads, no tracking and no permissions. Progress stays on your device, and the app only goes online for the App Store purchase. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I reset my child's progress? Use Reset Progress in the grown-ups area. Deleting the app also removes it.

Can I share a summary with a therapist or teacher? Yes. The grown-ups area has a Share summary button that opens the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share.

Which languages are supported? English and Bahasa Indonesia.
