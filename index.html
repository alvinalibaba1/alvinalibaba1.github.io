Splitin — Smart Receipt & Bill Splitter

Splitin turns a receipt into an editable split in seconds. Its privacy-first hybrid scanner uses on-device Vision and Apple Foundation Models when available, with an OpenAI-powered Cloudflare fallback for harder receipts. Users can split evenly or by item, handle tax, service charges and discounts, share polished summaries, and follow unpaid balances with history, statistics and reminders.

Key Features:

  • AI-assisted receipt parsing with printed-total reconciliation and editable results
  • Equal or per-item splitting with quantities, tax, service charges, and discounts
  • Paid-status tracking, local reminders, bill history, search, and collection statistics
  • Shareable text, image, and branded PDF summaries with payment details
  • English and Indonesian localization, dark mode, and accessibility-aware UI

Technology Stack:

SwiftUI Combine Vision VisionKit Foundation Models StoreKit 2 UserNotifications Cloudflare Workers D1 OpenAI

Soundboard Pop — Play Sounds with One Key

Soundboard Pop turns your Mac into a sound pad. Tap a tile, or press its key from any app, and the sound plays instantly, even while OBS, Discord or a game is in front. Built with Swift and SwiftUI, with a free tier and a Pro upgrade for creators.

Key Features:

  • System-wide hotkeys: give every tile its own key or combo, with duplicate-key protection
  • Low-latency, overlapping playback with loop, hold-to-play, fade out and a Stop All key
  • Waveform trimming, volume, emoji and color per tile; drag-and-drop import of mp3, wav, m4a and aiff
  • Multiple boards with search, tags, reordering, undo delete, automatic backups and shareable board files
  • Pro: mic mixing, per-tile output routing, sound chains, stop groups, pitch and speed
  • 24 built-in royalty-free sounds, a guided tour and an Easy Mode with bigger tiles

Technology Stack:

SwiftUI AVAudioEngine AVFoundation Carbon Hotkeys StoreKit 2 App Sandbox Swift Package Manager

SpecialKids

SpecialKids is a calm place for young children to practise matching, sorting, tracing, feelings and daily routines. It was made with autistic children in mind, and works well for any child who likes things quiet and predictable. Feedback uses soft sounds and pictures instead of talking, with no sad faces, red crosses or countdowns.

Key Features:

  • 7 wordless games, each with 12 themes: Jigsaw, Matching, Sorting, Tracing, Feelings, My Routine and Sound Match.
  • Sensory settings for grown-ups: music, sound effects, vibration, voice and reduced motion, or all at once with Quiet Mode.
  • Levels that move up when a child is ready and stay put when they need more practice.
  • A grown-ups area (hold to open) with play insights and a shareable summary for a therapist or teacher.
  • 3 calm picture stories and a Free Play mode with no goals and no scores.
  • No ads, no tracking, no data collected. Available in English and Bahasa Indonesia.

Technology Stack:

SwiftUI AVFoundation StoreKit 2
