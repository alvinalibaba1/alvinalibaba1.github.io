CopyDoc Support

Help with scanning, Pro, and your files.

Talk to us

Email alvinreyvaldo1@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version, and what happened. We usually reply within two working days.

Is CopyDoc free?

Scanning, cleaning, saving, sharing, folders, Face ID and basic signing are free, with no scan limit, no watermark and no ads. Copy Text, To Word, Mark Up, Protect, Merge, Pages, Make Smaller and search inside files are Pro tools. You get three free Pro tries before paying.

How do I restore CopyDoc Pro?

Open Account → See Premium, then tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought Pro with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CopyDoc, then cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and does not renew.

Where are my files?

Scans and PDFs stay on your iPhone, inside CopyDoc. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app removes the files it stored, so share anything you want to keep first.

Copy Text says I need Pro

Copy Text is a Pro tool. You can try it free three times across all Pro tools. If the three tries are used, open Account → See Premium to unlock everything.

Camera or Face ID does not work

Open iPhone Settings → CopyDoc and turn on Camera (or Face ID). CopyDoc only uses the camera while you are scanning. Face ID is optional.

Which iPhones are supported?

CopyDoc 1.0 needs iOS 16.4 or later. Camera scanning works on a real iPhone; the simulator cannot scan.
